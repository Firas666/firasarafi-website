Why brands bring me in

More than a designer

Most brands come looking for a designer. They leave with a partner who can design the product, lead the team behind it, and build the fundamentals that keep it growing.

01

As a product designer

I solve the product.

Research, flows, and interfaces that turn complex systems into products people understand, from royalty platforms to investment apps.

02

As CCO of FUBAR.asia

I deliver with a team.

A creative studio behind me for brand, web, and campaigns, so bigger launches never depend on one pair of hands.

03

As a design educator

I grow your people.

✦ Fun fact

Not every brand hires me to design.

Many brands do not come looking for a product designer, or even an agency. They come looking for an expert who can see the whole picture and tell them what to do next.

Because I am also an educator with a growing design network, what I bring goes beyond the deliverable: the plan behind it, the team that runs it, and the fundamentals that keep it going long after the project ends.

01The planDirection, roadmap, and priorities before a single screen gets designed.
02The teamFacilitating creative and product teams inside the company so they move faster, together.
03The fundamentalsProduct and design foundations, from design systems to workflows, that your team carries forward.

  • Strategy and roadmap sessions
  • Workshops and mentoring for your team

How it worksPer project or in sprints, working directly with your product team.

About Me

Designer & Educator

I'm a digital product designer based in Jakarta, passionate about creating meaningful experiences that blend aesthetics with functionality.

My Story

Background

Creative strategist and design leader with 8+ years shaping digital products, brand experiences, and design education. I build systems, curriculums, design teams, campaigns, and product workflows by combining product design principles with creative direction and experience design.

I led the School of Design at Purwadhika, overseeing Visual Design, UI/UX Design, and 3D Animation programs while developing industry partnerships with Thinking Room, Antikode, Agate, and other leading agencies. I'm also the Founder of FUBAR Studio, an experience-led creative studio delivering strategy, digital experiences, and brand activations for culture-shaping partners such as Magno, Ideafest, Massive Music, Lucy in the Sky, Finfolk, Menjadi Manusia, and Rotasi7.

My work focuses on crafting clarity, emotional resonance, and meaningful experiences across every touchpoint.
